# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

limes is released under **Apache-2.0** for its engine, with a **separate license
planned for the curated detection corpus and calibration** (see ADR 0004). To keep
that dual-license option alive, the project needs the right to license every
contribution under both — which a contributor's Apache-2.0 grant alone does not
provide. **No external contribution is merged without a signed CLA.** Without it,
the dual-license option dies the day the first external patch lands.

This agreement is adapted from the Apache Software Foundation Individual and
Corporate Contributor License Agreements, v2.1. It gives **the Project Owner,
Amadou Mamane ("the Owner"),** the rights described below. It does **not** change
your rights to use your own Contributions for any other purpose, and it does not
transfer ownership: you keep the copyright to your work.

There are two agreements. Sign the **Individual CLA** if you contribute on your
own behalf. Sign the **Corporate CLA** if you contribute as part of your work for
an employer, or if your employer has any rights to your contributions.

## How to sign

Signing is a **one-time** step. When You open Your first pull request, a maintainer
will confirm which of the following applies:

1. **Automated CLA check.** If a CLA-assistant check is enabled on the repository,
   follow its prompt on the pull request to record Your agreement.
2. **In-PR sign-off.** Otherwise, add a comment to the pull request with the
   statement below, filled in. For a Corporate CLA, an authorized signer of the
   Corporation must post it.

```
I have read the limes CLA (CLA.md) and I agree to it.

  Full name:
  GitHub username:
  Email:
  [ ] Individual   [ ] Corporate — Corporation name:
  Date:
```

Your agreement covers Your present and future Contributions to limes until You
revoke it in writing (a revocation is not retroactive).
